## Data Availability

The LAMMPS input files, random seeds, analysis scripts, processed data
underlying the figures and tables, and representative atomic configurations
supporting this study are publicly available at GitHub: **[GITHUB URL]**. A
versioned archival copy and the complete Cr50-S1 trajectory used for the
detailed structural analysis are available at Zenodo: **[ZENODO DOI]**. The
remaining full molecular-dynamics trajectories and per-displacement relaxation
dumps are not duplicated because of their approximately 620 GB aggregate size;
they can be regenerated from the deposited inputs and random seeds. The
complete original files are available from the corresponding author upon
reasonable request.
